Hi, my name is Niall McSheffrey. I'm a product leader at a health tech company, and from time to time I post technical musings about my work and what I see in the future of product management. I've been in product management for the last 15 years and experienced the full spectrum of what it means to be a product manager, from the early days of startups to the corporate world of building things fast and slow. 

Most people build a career by either starting things from scratch or scaling massive, complex machines. I’ve spent the last fifteen years obsessing over the bridge between the two—how to take a messy, zero-to-one idea and turn it into something that runs with mechanical precision. Maybe that comes from my background. I started out as an aerospce engineer after studying Mechanical Engineering at the University of Kansas before earning an MBA in Global Management at Thunderbird. That combination—how systems physically work under the hood, paired with how businesses scale across borders—has shaped every product I’ve touched since.


My first real proving ground was founding Elementz Interactive. What started as a solo venture grew over a decade into a 50-person global product studio spanning Santa Monica and Cebu in the Philippines. Acting as  CPO and CTO, I helped ship over 40 digital products across healthcare, fintech, manufacturing, and enterprise SaaS—generating more than $10M in software sales and helping two client products reach Series C and D funding rounds. Building across continents and industries taught me early on that great product leadership isn’t about rigid playbooks; it’s about adaptability, empathy for the end user, and ruthless clarity on outcomes.


Over the past several years, I’ve brought that builder’s mindset into high-growth healthcare technology, tackling some of the thorniest operational and clinical problems in the industry. At SimplePractice, I led the transformation of clinician analytics and EHR dashboards, cutting development costs in half while doubling customer engagement. I bring deep expertise in data science, data visualization, and user experience.


Today, as a Group Product Manager at Spring Health (formerly Alma), I operate at the intersection of massive financial infrastructure and rapid zero-to-one growth. On one side, my squads run the Revenue Cycle Management and eligibility engine—a system processing over $1 billion and 100 million claims annually, where our automation work has saved over $10M in revenue adjustments and cut claim costs by 25%. On the other side, I have led zero-to-one tiger teams focused on care delivery and ePrescribing, accelerated provider activation to unlock tens of thousands of new monthly patient visits. 

Alongside my day job, I also advise InkCloud, helping guide product and go-to-market strategy for an ERP startup that has doubled its revenue year-over-year.


Whether I’m prototyping with modern AI tools like Claude Code, untangling complex APIs, or aligning multiple squads around a single north star, my philosophy remains the same: strip away the friction, respect the people using the software, and build systems that scale.


When I’m not deep in product roadmaps or architecture, you can find me in Southern California, jamming on guitar with my favortite cover band, Echo Riders.
