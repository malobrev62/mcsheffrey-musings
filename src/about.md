I'm Niall. I was born in Ireland, my family is from Coleraine, and I came to America at fifteen. I've spent my working life in product, most of it in health tech, and I live in Orange County now.

This is where I write about the things that take a while to understand: music that builds, places with history in them, the people I meet, and the long project of working out where I come from.

New posts land here when they're ready.
